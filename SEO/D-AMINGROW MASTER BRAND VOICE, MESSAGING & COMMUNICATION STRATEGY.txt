# 1. BRAND FOUNDATION

## 1.1 Who We Are

D-AminGrow is an agro-allied company providing livestock health products, poultry inputs, farm equipment, technical guidance and livestock-related solutions.

The company serves farmers, poultry operators, livestock businesses, agricultural entrepreneurs and individuals seeking dependable products and practical support for their farming operations.

Its product and service areas include:

- Day-old broiler, pullet and turkey chicks.
- Point-of-lay birds.
- Livestock vaccines.
- Veterinary drugs and animal health products.
- Antibiotics, probiotics and nutritional supplements.
- Poultry and livestock housing accessories.
- Farm equipment and rearing supplies.
- Technical consultation and livestock management guidance.

D-AminGrow should communicate as a business that understands the practical realities of farming: animal health, feeding, housing, disease prevention, flock management, product selection, farm productivity and the importance of informed decisions.

## 1.2 Brand Purpose

To make dependable livestock solutions and practical agricultural knowledge more accessible to farmers, helping them build healthier livestock communities and more sustainable farming operations.

## 1.3 Brand Vision

To contribute to a stronger, healthier and more competitive African livestock sector through accessible livestock solutions, practical knowledge and responsible agricultural support.

## 1.4 Brand Mission

To support farmers and livestock businesses with relevant products, dependable service, practical technical guidance and useful information that helps them make better livestock management decisions.

## 1.5 Brand Promise

**We help farmers access the livestock solutions, practical knowledge and support they need to make more informed farming decisions.**

## 1.6 Brand Motto

**Your satisfaction is our priority.**

This motto should be expressed through the actual customer experience: clear communication, transparent product information, responsible recommendations, responsive support and dependable service.

It must not become an empty slogan.

# 6. THE D-AMINGROW VOICE

The brand voice is the consistent personality behind everything D-AminGrow publishes.

## 6.1 Voice Principle One: Educate Before You Sell

Do not merely announce that a product is available.

Explain what it is, what it is generally used for, who may need it and what customers should consider before purchasing.

**Weak:**
"We sell quality poultry vaccines. Contact us today."

**Preferred:**
"Protecting flock health begins with a well-planned vaccination programme. Explore suitable poultry vaccine options and speak with our team about the products and guidance relevant to your flock."

## 6.2 Voice Principle Two: Be Confident, Not Exaggerated

Avoid statements that cannot be substantiated.

**Weak:**
"Our vaccines guarantee 100% protection against every poultry disease."

**Preferred:**
"Vaccination is an important component of poultry disease prevention. The appropriate programme depends on factors such as bird type, age, health status, local disease risks and professional veterinary guidance."

## 6.3 Voice Principle Three: Be Practical, Not Abstract

Every educational message should help the reader understand something useful.

**Weak:**
"Good poultry management is essential for productivity."

**Preferred:**
"Clean drinking water, suitable ventilation, appropriate stocking density and consistent monitoring are important parts of responsible poultry management."

## 6.4 Voice Principle Four: Be Human, Not Robotic

Write as a knowledgeable person speaking to another person.

**Weak:**
"Customers are advised to utilise our livestock management solutions."

**Preferred:**
"Need help choosing the right livestock supplies for your farm? Tell us about your birds and farming needs, and our team can help you explore suitable options."

## 6.5 Voice Principle Five: Be Commercial Without Being Pushy

The brand should make it easy for customers to take the next step without creating unnecessary pressure.

**Preferred CTA examples:**
- Request Current Prices.
- Enquire About Availability.
- Book Your Day-Old Chicks.
- Speak With Our Team.
- Explore Livestock Solutions.
- Get Product Information.
- Request Technical Guidance.

# 7. BRAND WRITING DNA

## 7.1 Sentence Structure

Use a combination of:

- Short sentences for clarity and emphasis.
- Medium-length sentences for explanations.
- Longer sentences when detailed technical context is necessary.

Avoid paragraphs that contain too many unrelated ideas.

## 7.2 Paragraph Structure

A good D-AminGrow paragraph should generally:

1. Introduce a farming concern or customer need.
2. Explain why it matters.
3. Offer practical information or a relevant solution.
4. Identify the next useful action.

## 7.3 Preferred Communication Pattern

**Problem → Understanding → Practical Guidance → Relevant Solution → Action**

Example:

"Choosing day-old chicks is an important decision for any poultry farmer. Breed selection, intended production purpose, housing preparation and access to appropriate technical guidance all matter. D-AminGrow provides day-old broiler, pullet and turkey chick enquiries to help customers explore available options. Contact our team to request current availability and pricing."

## 7.4 Use Active Voice

Prefer:
- "Our team helps farmers explore suitable livestock products."
- "Request the current price."
- "Prepare your poultry house before the chicks arrive."

Avoid unnecessary passive constructions.

## 7.5 Avoid Empty Adjectives

Words such as "best", "number one", "world-class", "unbeatable", "premium" and "revolutionary" should only be used when they communicate a specific, defensible distinction.

Prefer specific product attributes and verifiable information.

# 12. VETERINARY AND ANIMAL HEALTH COMMUNICATION POLICY

This section is mandatory.

D-AminGrow operates in a category where careless language can affect animal welfare, customer finances and public trust.

All animal-health communication must prioritise accuracy, responsible product information and appropriate professional guidance.

## 12.1 Never Diagnose Animals Through Generic Marketing Content

Do not tell customers that a symptom definitely means a particular disease.

Instead, explain that similar symptoms may have different causes and recommend appropriate veterinary assessment.

## 12.2 Do Not Prescribe Treatment Casually

Do not generate medication dosage, treatment schedules, antibiotic combinations or vaccination schedules without reliable product-specific and veterinary grounding.

## 12.3 Respect Product Labels

All medicine and vaccine information must be checked against the actual product label and relevant manufacturer documentation.

## 12.4 Communicate Responsible Antibiotic Use

Antibiotic content should promote responsible veterinary use and awareness of antimicrobial resistance.

Avoid encouraging routine or preventive antibiotic use without appropriate professional justification.

## 12.5 Never Promise Disease Elimination

Vaccines and medicines have specific indications, limitations, administration requirements and storage conditions.

Never promise complete immunity, guaranteed survival or zero mortality.

## 12.6 Cold-Chain and Storage Claims

Do not claim that a product is properly refrigerated, temperature-monitored or cold-chain certified unless the company has documented procedures and evidence supporting the claim.

## 12.7 Product Registration and Regulatory Claims

Do not describe a product as NAFDAC-approved or registered unless its actual regulatory status has been verified.

Verify product-specific registration details against authoritative records and documentation.

Relevant regulatory reference: NAFDAC's veterinary product registration information.

# 14. SEO BRAND VOICE

SEO should strengthen the brand's communication, not distort it.

D-AminGrow must write primarily for people while making its content understandable to search engines.

## 14.1 SEO Writing Principles

- Address genuine search intent.
- Demonstrate first-hand experience where available.
- Publish accurate, useful and original information.
- Use clear headings and logical content structure.
- Include relevant product and service information.
- Explain technical terms.
- Cite reliable sources when appropriate.
- Identify authors and qualified reviewers where relevant.
- Update time-sensitive information.
- Avoid mass-produced, repetitive pages.

## 14.2 Keyword Integration

Use relevant search terms naturally, including:

- Livestock products in Nigeria.
- Poultry vaccines in Nigeria.
- Day-old chicks in Nigeria.
- Broiler chicks for sale.
- Pullet chicks for sale.
- Turkey poults.
- Point-of-lay birds in Nigeria.
- Poultry farming equipment.
- Veterinary drugs for livestock.
- Poultry farming supplies.
- Livestock health products.
- Poultry farming consultation.
- Livestock management guidance.

These are keyword themes, not verified search-volume or ranking opportunities. Validate actual demand, competition and intent before prioritising them.

## 14.3 Search-Friendly Content Structure

A useful educational article should include:

1. Clear, descriptive title.
2. Direct answer to the searcher's question.
3. Short introduction.
4. Detailed explanation.
5. Practical steps or considerations.
6. Common mistakes.
7. Safety or professional guidance where appropriate.
8. Frequently asked questions where they add value.
9. Relevant internal links.
10. Author and reviewer information where appropriate.

## 14.4 Avoid SEO Manipulation

Never:

- Stuff keywords unnaturally.
- Create hundreds of near-identical location pages.
- Publish invented veterinary advice.
- Copy competitors' articles.
- Generate large volumes of low-value AI content.
- Invent author credentials.
- Create fake reviews or testimonials.
- Use misleading structured data.

The objective is to earn visibility by being genuinely useful.
